This is actually a very interesting opportunity, Richard.

The existing academic reference managers each solved part of the problem:

EndNote = enterprise power, publishing workflows
Zotero = openness, research collection, browser capture
Mendeley = PDF-centric reading and annotation
Papers/ReadCube = modern discovery, recommendations, reading experience
Your advantage is that Paper-inator does not need to be a clone. It can be designed around your existing Hub -> Module architecture and focus on a coherent researcher workflow.

My recommendation is:

Book-inator manages books.

Paper-inator manages knowledge sources, academic literature, citations, and research projects.
