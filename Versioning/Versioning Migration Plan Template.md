# **📄 Migration Plan Template**

## 1. Standard
Link to the **policy or standard** being adopted (e.g., Versioning Policy).

---

## 2. Migration Owner
- Team Lead or responsible steward.  
- Contact information for escalation.

---

## 3. Migration Timeline
- **Start Date**: YYYY‑MM‑DD  
- **Target Completion Date**: YYYY‑MM‑DD  
- **Milestones**: Key checkpoints (e.g., draft adoption, pilot rollout, full enforcement).

---

## 4. Scope
- Systems, repositories, or components affected.  
- Teams or stakeholders impacted.  
- Dependencies on external services or APIs.

---

## 5. Migration Steps
- Step‑by‑step actions required to adopt the standard.  
- Include automation scripts, CI/CD enforcement, or manual updates.  
- Each step MUST be traceable to a changelog entry.

---

## 6. Risks & Mitigations
- Identify risks (e.g., breaking builds, dependency conflicts).  
- Define mitigation strategies (rollback, compatibility layers, phased rollout).

---

## 7. Dependencies
- Technical dependencies (libraries, infrastructure).  
- Organizational dependencies (approvals, cross‑team coordination).

---

## 8. Communication Plan
- Notify stakeholders of upcoming changes.  
- Provide documentation, training, or migration guides.  
- Define escalation paths for blockers.

---

## 9. Metrics
- Define success criteria (e.g., % of repos migrated, CI compliance rate).  
- Track adoption progress with measurable indicators.

---

## 10. Blockers
- Document blockers and escalation paths.  
- Assign responsibility for resolution.

---

## 11. Status Updates
- Provide periodic updates (weekly/bi‑weekly).  
- Include progress reports and issues encountered.

---

## 12. Completion Criteria
- Define what “migration complete” means (e.g., all repos compliant, CI enforcement active).  
- Steward verification required before closure.

---

## 13. Changelog
- Track revisions to this migration plan.

---

⚡ This template ensures migrations are **predictable, traceable, and enforceable**.
